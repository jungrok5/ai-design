<Intro>

React is the result of years of work by many people. This page recognizes some of the individuals and groups who have helped make it what it is today.

</Intro>

## Origins {/*origins*/}

React was originally created by Jordan Walke at Facebook, now Meta. It was first used in the News Feed in 2011 and on Instagram in 2012. It was open-sourced at JSConf US in May 2013. Many engineers at the company contributed to its early design, testing, and adoption, often while building products that depended on it.

## The React team {/*the-react-team*/}

Today, React is developed by a dedicated team at Meta, together with contributors from other companies and from the open-source community. We are grateful to every past and present member of the team. Their work covers the core library, the compiler, the developer tools, the documentation, and the many experiments that never shipped but shaped the ideas that did.

## Open source contributors {/*open-source-contributors*/}

Thousands of people have contributed to React over the years. They have:

* Fixed bugs and improved performance
* Written and translated documentation
* Reported issues with clear reproduction steps
* Reviewed pull requests and answered questions
* Built libraries, frameworks, and tools on top of React

You can see the full list of contributors on the [React repository on GitHub](https://github.com/facebook/react/graphs/contributors).

## Community {/*community*/}

React would not be what it is without its community. Thank you to the educators who teach it, the conference organizers who bring people together, and the maintainers of the wider ecosystem. Thank you also to everyone who has asked a question, shared a mistake, or helped a newcomer get unstuck.

## Inspiration {/*inspiration*/}

React builds on ideas from many other projects and fields, including functional programming, declarative user interface research, and earlier web libraries. We thank the authors of that work for sharing it openly.
